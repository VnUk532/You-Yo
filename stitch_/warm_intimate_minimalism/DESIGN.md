## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an intimate, safe, and emotionally resonant sanctuary for couples. Grounded in cozy romantic minimalism, it balances modern clarity with tactile warmth. The emotional objective is to evoke tenderness, shared history, and quiet joy without descending into kitsch or juvenile sentimentality. 

The aesthetic is characterized by:
- **Atmospheric Warmth**: Generous ivory and cream fields that mimic organic cotton and fine vellum paper, banishing sterile stark white.
- **Tender Tactility**: Ultra-soft radii, pill-shaped interactions, and whisper-quiet frosted glass panels that create layers of intimacy and depth.
- **Restraint & Presence**: Elegant editorial serif headings paired with clean, human geometric body text, allowing photographs, personal notes, and shared milestones to become the focal points.
- **Micro-Delight**: Subtly glowing badges, quiet heart/sparkle vector accents, and organic pill containers that frame shared experiences with reverence.

## Colors

The palette revolves around skin-warmed neutrals, blushing rose tones, earthy terracotta, and balancing sage accents.

- **Primary (`#FF6B8B` - Blush Rose)**: Used for high-affinity romantic touchpoints, active states, key CTAs, and heart emblems. Expresses affection, vitality, and connection.
- **Secondary (`#FA7070` - Tender Terracotta)**: Used for emotional milestones, anniversary accents, memory highlights, and warm gradient fills when paired with Blush Rose.
- **Tertiary (`#6E9885` - Muted Sage)**: Used purposefully for utility surfaces, calendar tracking, scheduled dates, checklists, and neutral balance points to keep the interface grounding and practical.
- **Neutral Core (`#2D2926` - Soft Charcoal)**: Replaces harsh true black for body copy and headings, offering high contrast with a warm, ink-on-paper feel.
- **Backgrounds & Surfaces**: Built from warm ivory (`#FDFBF7`) as the root backdrop, fading into soft blush cream (`#FFF8F5`) for primary cards and elevated containers.
- **Color Shifts & Translucency**: Accent colors operate with low-opacity variants (8–14%) for tinted chip backgrounds, subtle borders, and delicate halo shadows.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial, intimate mood while maintaining effortless legibility on mobile screens.

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (`Newsreader`)**: Expresses warmth, narrative storytelling, literary romance, and timeless elegance. Used for daily greetings, timeline dates, anniversary countdowns, and question-of-the-day headers.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides a friendly, clear, modern counterweight. Its open counters and geometric balance ensure readability in chat threads, shared to-do lists, and calendar slots.
- **Letter Spacing & Pairing**: Headline serifs use slight negative tracking for an intimate, crafted feel. Small labels utilize subtle positive tracking for crispness against tinted pastel badges.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for fluid mobile-first ergonomics with generous vertical breathing room, honoring touch targets (minimum 48×48px) and thumb-reach zones.

- **Grid & Margins**: A 4-column layout on mobile devices with `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins, scaling to 8 columns on tablets with `2rem` margins. The content remains centered with a max-width container on larger screens to preserve intimacy.
- **Spacing Rhythm**: Governed by a base 4px/8px scale. Dense list items and internal chip padding apply `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while content blocks and grouped cards rely on `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px). Major emotional sections (e.g., memory albums, love letter reveals) enjoy `space-xl` (36px) to avoid claustrophobic crowding.
- **Safe Areas**: Strict inclusion of OS-level bottom home bars and top status indicators, with frosted bottom navigation floating slightly above the canvas bottom margin.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses frosted glassmorphism layered over tender pastel ambient shadows, completely eliminating harsh drop shadows or stark black overlays.

- **Base Layer (Surface 0)**: Warm ivory canvas (`#FDFBF7`).
- **Card Layer (Surface 1)**: Solid or translucent warm ivory-pink (`rgba(255, 248, 245, 0.85)`) coupled with a backdrop-blur of `16px` to `24px`. Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 107, 139, 0.12)`. Ambient shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(250, 112, 112, 0.08)`.
- **Raised & Floating Layer (Surface 2 - Modals, Snackbars, Floating Nav)**: Frosted glass (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.92)`) with a `20px` blur, bordered with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`. Soft diffuse drop shadow: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.06), 0 4px 12px rgba(255, 107, 139, 0.08)`.
- **Micro Accents**: Subtle radial gradients positioned off-center behind hero moments emit a blush-terracotta glow, softly diffusing through frosted cards.

## Shapes

The design system embraces deep, organic curvature to evoke softness, comfort, and emotional accessibility.

- **Large Panels & Cards**: Use generous radii (`24px` to `32px` / `rounded-3xl` and `rounded-2xl`), evoking river stones or plush cushions.
- **Interactive Elements & Pills**: Buttons, badges, quick reaction capsules, and status indicators are fully pill-shaped (`9999px`), inviting tactile interaction.
- **Dividers & Micro Shapes**: Avoid sharp 1px lines; separators are rendered as soft 4px dotted pill tracks or delicate tapered lines with fading opacity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button**: Pill-shaped container filled with a subtle gradient from Blush Rose (`#FF6B8B`) to Tender Terracotta (`#FA7070`). Label in high-contrast crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a small trailing heart or sparkle icon. Ambient glow shadow on active state (`0 8px 20px rgba(255, 107, 139, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Button**: Ivory/cream fill with a delicate tinted border (`1.5px solid rgba(255, 107, 139, 0.25)`), text styled in Soft Charcoal (`#2D2926`).
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Borderless, displaying Soft Charcoal or Blush Rose text with a faint blush ripple on touch.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped with internal padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Romantic tags (e.g., "Our Anniversary", "Date Night") use 10% Blush Rose fill with solid `#FF6B8B` text.
- Utility tags (e.g., "Grocery", "Synchronized", "Tomorrow") use 12% Sage (`#6E9885`) fill with deep `#4D6D5F` text.

### Cards
- **Memory Card**: Translucent warm cream card with `24px` rounded corners, thin glass border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), containing full-bleed photo corners, newsreader title, and interactive heart toggle.
- **Question of the Day Card**: Floating frosted card with subtle terracotta inner glow, inviting paired answers with split avatars at the base.

### Inputs & Fields
- Generous 52px height pills or 20px rounded containers. Background tinted cream (`#FFF8F5`), border in neutral gray-rose (`rgba(45, 41, 38, 0.1)`). Focus state transitions to `1.5px solid #FF6B8B` accompanied by a soft 4px blush halo ring.

### Checkboxes & Toggles
- Custom circular checkbox for shared to-dos. When checked, morphs into a Blush Rose or Sage filled circle displaying a crisp white heart or check icon.
- Toggles utilize smooth pill capsules with soft cream thumbs that slightly scale up on press.

### Lists
- Borderless rows separated by `space-sm` gaps rather than hard dividers. Each list item functions as an individual mini-card with soft-touch states (`rgba(255, 107, 139, 0.05)`).

### Specialized Couple Components
- **Dual-Avatar Intersect**: Overlapping circular avatars with a glowing heart badge anchored at the intersection point to indicate mutual presence.
- **Mood Slider**: Horizontal pill slider with gradient track (Sage to Rose) indicating emotional temperature or affection levels.
